## Judge Notes

- This is a live-scored mission.
- [[ON TOP OF]] is determined using the [[ON TOP OF]] definition.
- Support from a robot is permitted.
- The cubes do not need to remain [[ON TOP OF]] the Large Red Cube after scoring.
- Both Small Red Cubes must simultaneously satisfy the [[ON TOP OF]] definition to score the Bonus Mission.
