Discovery Projects

Two strands you can work through independently. Everything you build gets tested against a real Stack Attack mission.

Strand One Coding Connect to your Wombat, make it move, make it grab, make it sense. Written in KISS-C, one project at a time, each one anchored to a mission you can actually score. 17 projects · 6 phases · complete Open Coding → Strand Two Systems How things get designed, how machines and people pass information back and forth, how to stay safe online, and how to work as a team that finishes what it starts. 14 projects · 5 phases · in progress Open Systems →
Which one first? Either. The strands do not depend on each other — pick whichever your team is ready for. Watch for the badges on the Systems cards — a few of them need doing before certain Coding projects, and the claw build is a hard gate before Coding Project 7.
