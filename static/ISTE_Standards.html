=== METHODOLOGY (revised) ===

This crosswalk scores against FULL LAB BODIES: reflection prompts, Individual Reflection phases, AI Literacy Bridge sections, and Extension Challenges. Evidence base is 750 reflection prompts and 255 Extension Challenges across 27 C labs, 27 Python labs, and 31 Discovery projects.

An earlier revision of this file scored lab FRONT MATTER ONLY (headings, descriptions, concept tags, objectives) and materially undercounted coverage. Those figures are superseded.

Scoring rule: Full requires evidence in the core lab flow. Partial is assigned where the only evidence sits in an optional Extension Challenge (labelled 'Finished early?' in the labs), or where the standard is only partly met. Cells citing 'Ext N.NL' identify the specific extension carrying the evidence, so a reviewer can verify placement and decide whether optional work counts for their purposes.
